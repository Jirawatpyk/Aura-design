# Theming and locale — props

A brand colour per project, and Thai, English or Swedish built-in labels. Tables are generated from the TypeScript sources of `@jirawatpyk/aura-react`, so they always match the published types. `*` = required.

## AuraProvider

<ArgTypes of={Aura.AuraProvider} />

`locale="sv"` gives Swedish labels, `sv-SE` dates, the Gregorian calendar and Monday as the first day. Only `th` shows Buddhist-era years (pass `calendar` to override). Without a provider, components are English with Gregorian dates. `linkComponent={Link}` (from `next/link`) makes every AURA link — Button `href`, Breadcrumb, Pagination `getHref`, Stat `href`, SideNav and DataTable row and pager links — use client-side navigation.

## Chart colours

`--aura-chart-1` … `--aura-chart-8` for series, in this order: violet, teal, orange, blue, magenta, green, gold, rose. `--aura-chart-seq-1` … `-5` for ordered values (low → high), `--aura-chart-grid` and `--aura-chart-axis`. Every mark colour is ≥3:1 on the surface in both themes and neighbours alternate dark and mid lightness, so series next to each other stay apart without hue. `createTheme` moves chart-1 and the sequential ramp to the brand. Label series in the legend or on the chart as well; colour is never the only cue. With Recharts: `<Bar fill="var(--aura-chart-1)" />`.

## ColorSchemeScript

Put it in `<head>`: it applies the saved choice (or `system`) before the page paints, so there is no flash of the wrong scheme. `useColorScheme()` returns `{ scheme, resolved, setScheme }` for your own controls.

<ArgTypes of={Aura.ColorSchemeScript} />

With a nonce-based Content-Security-Policy pass the request's nonce: `<ColorSchemeScript nonce={nonce} />`.
